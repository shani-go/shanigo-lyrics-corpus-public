LicenseRef-dotstokyo-PD-Grant — the dots.tokyo public-domain grant

Applies to: songs/song_34.txt, songs/song_35.txt

The idol group ・・・・・・・・・ (dotstokyo) published its recordings and
lyrics as public domain on its own page, announced on 2019-04-12 by the
group's management. The page lets anyone use the recordings and lyrics,
including modified, secondary and commercial use, with no permission,
reporting, payment or attribution. Its one request is that nobody impair
their public-domain status by claiming exclusive rights in the unmodified
material. A condition about choreography does not concern the lyrics. The
operative Japanese wording is quoted verbatim in songs/song_34.rights.md
(rights_basis:).

  Grant:          https://dots.tokyo/PD/
  Archived copy:  http://web.archive.org/web/20190415124804/https://dots.tokyo/PD/
                  (2019-04-15, identical to the live page as read on
                  2026-09-29)

Japan does not recognise abandonment of copyright, so the grant operates
there as an irrevocable, royalty-free permission rather than as a
public-domain status. The grant is the management's, not signed by the
individual lyricist; see the caveat in NOTICE.md. Attribution
("作詞 みきれちゃん / ・・・・・・・・・, dots.tokyo/PD/") is given as a courtesy.
