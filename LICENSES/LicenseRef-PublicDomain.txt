its country of origin, and in every other country under every known term

copyright term has run.

The basis for each file, with its evidence, is in that song's rights record
(songs/song_<n>.rights.md). It is summarised in RIGHTS_LEDGER.md and in the
file's copyright text in REUSE.toml. Regimes outside copyright that can bear
on commercial use in particular countries (folklore laws, an edition layer)
are described in NOTICE.md.

The determination is not legal advice. Laws differ by country, and the
records carry their evidence so that a reader can check it.
